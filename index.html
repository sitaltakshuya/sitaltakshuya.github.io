A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I'm Sital Sharma, a computer science professional with research interests spanning artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, and data analytics.

My work explores practical AI systems, with a particular interest in bringing language technology to low-resource languages such as Nepali. I enjoy moving between research questions and working prototypes — from language models and voice interfaces to computer vision and data-intensive systems.

I also enjoy teaching technical concepts in a clear, hands-on way, using worked examples, experiments, and projects that connect theory to practice.
